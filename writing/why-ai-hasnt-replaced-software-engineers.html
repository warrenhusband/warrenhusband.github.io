Why AI hasn’t replaced software engineers, and won’t

November 12, 2026

The eightfold increase in code generation versus a 30% increase in software releases is a pretty telling statistic. We're getting much better at producing things. That doesn't necessarily mean we're getting better at producing the right things. I suspect that gap between generation and verification will define a lot of the next phase of enterprise AI.
