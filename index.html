Warren Husband

CTO, EdgeTheory

Warren Husband is the Chief Technology Officer at EdgeTheory, a narrative intelligence company, where he leads the engineering team.

Narrative intelligence is about understanding the stories that shape what people believe: where they start, who pushes them, how they spread and where they're heading. There is far more online content than any team can read, and more of it is synthetic or coordinated every year. The goal is to spot a narrative early enough that the people making decisions, in government or in business, can act before it takes hold.

Warren has worked in software since 1995 as an engineer, team leader, IT director and CTO. Before joining EdgeTheory in 2018, he spent much of that time building systems for healthcare and medical labs. He's more of a people person than the typical engineer, and he likes keeping the business goal in view above the details. He lives in Mississippi.
