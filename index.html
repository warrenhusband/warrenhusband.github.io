Warren Husband is the Chief Technology Officer at EdgeTheory, a narrative intelligence company, where he leads the engineering team.

Narrative intelligence is the work of understanding the stories that shape what people believe: where they start, who pushes them, how they spread, and where they're heading. With more content online than any team can read, and more of it synthetic or coordinated every year, the goal is to spot a narrative early enough for decision-makers in government and business to act before it takes hold.

Warren has worked in software since 1995 as an engineer, team leader, IT director and CTO. Before joining EdgeTheory in 2018, he spent much of that time building systems for healthcare and medical labs. He enjoys working with people as much as with code, and he keeps the business goal in view above the technical details. He lives in Mississippi.
